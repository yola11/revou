STYLE

BORN IN
THE STREETS.

We didn't start in a boardroom. We started on corners, in skate parks, and in the back of trains — drawing on secondhand notebooks, photographing friends, and obsessing over the details that mass fashion always misses.

Every piece is designed to be worn, not preserved. The garments are built for those who live in them — who layer, distress, archive, and eventually pass them on.

This is not about trends. This is about permanence.
